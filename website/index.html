FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know accounting to use Kuza?

No. Kuza posts balanced double-entry journals automatically whenever you sell, buy or get paid. You run your business; the books keep themselves. If you have an accountant, they'll love the clean, exportable ledger.

Is Kuza Menu really free?

Yes — free forever on the Free plan, with a small "Powered by Kuza" footer. Create a QR menu and shareable link in minutes. Paid plans remove the branding and unlock table-side ordering, books and payments.

What does the 14-day trial include?

The Growth plan, in full — automatic books, invoicing, payment collection and per-branch P&L. No card required to start. If you don't upgrade, you simply move down to the Free plan and keep your data.

How do payments and reconciliation work?

Every invoice can carry a pay link (card or bank transfer via Paystack). When a customer pays, Kuza matches the payment to the invoice and posts it to your books automatically — no manual reconciliation.

Can I use Kuza across multiple branches?

Yes. Stock, sales and books are tracked per branch, and Enterprise adds consolidated group reporting across all of them. See profit per day, per branch, from one login.

Can I export my data if I leave?

Always. Full data export is free forever, on every plan including Free. Your data is yours — we'd rather you stay because the product is good, not because you're locked in.
